Questions

How much does a cleaning cost?

It depends on your space and the service. New clients get a free walkthrough and estimate, so you'll know the price before we start.

What areas do you serve?

We serve the Texas Hill Country. Not sure if you're in our area? Call or text us and we'll let you know.

Do I need to be home?

No. Many clients leave a key or a door code. Just let us know when you book.

Do you bring cleaning supplies?

Yes, we bring what we need. If you'd like us to use your own products, tell us when you book.

Do you clean Airbnbs and rentals?

Yes. We handle turnovers between guests so your place is ready for the next check-in.

How do I change or cancel a visit?

Call or text us as early as you can and we'll move your visit.
